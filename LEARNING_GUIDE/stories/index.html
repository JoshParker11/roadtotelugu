What N means here

Introduced is not the same as mastered.

The story generator may use language explicitly covered by completed lessons, including taught inflected forms. It does not claim every item is easy yet. Your personal Anki maturity remains separate from the course-controlled inventory.

01 / Controlled

Course language

Words, forms, and grammar explicitly introduced in a lesson. These make up the story’s N.

02 / New

One deliberate addition

At the foundation, each story adds at most one lexical item and no unannounced grammar pattern.

03 / Exposed

Bonus language stays outside

Words seen in books, songs, or deep dives do not enter controlled stories until intentionally promoted.

Generation contract

How future stories stay honest

Before a story is added, its words and grammar are audited against the ledger. Proper names are labeled and excluded from lexical novelty; taught suffix combinations count as forms, not mystery vocabulary.

Coverage

Aim for 90–95% controlled running tokens at the very beginning, then increase toward easier extensive input as the ledger grows.

Novelty

One new lexical item per foundation story. A new grammar pattern requires its own explanation and should not be smuggled in as vocabulary.

Forms

Deliberately repeat singular/plural, near/far, register, and known suffix forms so exposure varies without inflating the word count.

Verification

Generated Telugu remains draft instructional material until its naturalness and audio are checked by a proficient or native speaker.
