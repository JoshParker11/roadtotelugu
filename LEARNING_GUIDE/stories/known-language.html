Important distinction

“Introduced” means eligible for tightly controlled practice. “Mastered” is learner-specific and should eventually come from retrieval and comprehension evidence. The ledger is not a copy of the Anki deck: it groups taught forms such as vidyārthulu under a lexeme, while also recording language such as aṇḍi that appeared inside a lesson example without receiving its own card.
