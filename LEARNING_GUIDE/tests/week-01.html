Week 1 test — everything so far

Lessons 1, 2, 3, 5 and 6. Family terms are deliberately left out. Most of this asks you to produce Telugu rather than recognise it, because recognising a form you cannot yet say is the failure mode this whole course is built to avoid.

  • Say every answer aloud before you type it. The typing is for grading; the speaking is the point.
  • Write romanization, not script. Diacritics are optional — nenu vidyarthini marks the same as nēnu vidyārthini.
  • Nothing is timed and nothing is saved to a server. Your last score is kept in this browser so you can compare next time.
  • If the grader marks you wrong but you were right, use the override button. Telugu has more than one good answer and the grader only knows the ones I gave it.
