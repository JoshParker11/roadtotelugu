The spine of the course

Sentence stems, in English first

The frames that recur constantly in everyday speech, before any Telugu is attached. Review them, pick the ones that match your life, and everything else — grammar, vocabulary, drills — gets sourced from what you select.

Why English first

What people say every day barely changes across languages. Deciding which frames matter is a content question, not a Telugu question — so it's worth settling before translation muddies it. Once you've picked, the Telugu for most of these already exists in Lessons 1–6 or arrives with the tense lessons.

Tap any stem to select it. Your picks are saved in this browser. Export the list when you're done and I'll build the Telugu, the drills, and the cards around exactly that set.
