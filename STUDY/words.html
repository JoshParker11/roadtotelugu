Backup, and moving between devices

Marks are stored in this browser only, so your phone and your laptop keep separate lists — the same caveat the Verb Lab carries. Export here, paste there.

Words are keyed by a hash of their Telugu script rather than a row number, so an export stays valid across a rebuild of the deck: re-run the pipeline, re-import, and every mark still points at the word it was made about.

Where this data comes from

Generated from data/master_words.tsv by tools/build_studydata.py — the same master that produces the Anki import, so this list and the deck cannot drift apart. Last generated .

Held-out rows are shown but carry no position: bound endings, words still missing their Telugu script, and English words respelled in Telugu letters. They are excluded from the deck by tools/sequence.py, and hiding them here would make the page quietly disagree with the master about how many words exist.
