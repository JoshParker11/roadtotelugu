How the matching works, and where it is guessing

Words are resolved against the word master before the page is built. An exact match is reliable. A match marked like this is approximate: the text and the deck use different romanization conventions — adhi against adi, kooda against kūḍā — and the two are collapsed onto one skeleton to match at all. Usually right, occasionally not, so it is flagged rather than trusted.

Marking a word known writes to the same store the vocabulary queue uses, so it also unlocks sentences in the drill. That only happens for words that exist in the master; anything else has no deck identity yet, which is exactly why it is worth mining.
