What is generated and what is not

Every sentence here came from a source. In transform mode the verb is swapped for another cell of the same verb, produced by the Verb Lab's engine — so the sentence is real and the swapped form is one the Lab already drills, but that exact sentence is assembled rather than attested. Answers say so.

Transformations that would break the sentence are not offered: the imperatives and “let's” delete the subject, wantTo needs a dative subject, and the conditional makes a fragment. Person swaps are only offered where there is a bare pronoun subject to move — otherwise ikkaḍa okaṭi undi would become nēnu ikkaḍa okaṭi unnānu, which is grammatical and nonsense. Verbs whose tense labels are known to lie only offer the cells whose meaning has been pinned down.
