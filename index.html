Where this is honest with you

Telugu here is draft until a native speaker sees it

The course lessons are extracted from an external resource. The mini story, the assembled sentence scripts, and every verb paradigm in the Verb Lab were generated and have not been checked by a proficient Telangana speaker. Individual forms cross-check against each other, which is reassuring about the method, not proof about any given cell.

The Verb Lab's review page exists for exactly this: six questions settle a verb, and a correction to one stem fixes its whole paradigm.

Register

Everything targets educated colloquial Hyderabad/Telangana speech. A coastal speaker or a textbook will differ in places — chebutānu against cheptānu, -āmu against -āṁ. Neither is wrong. Follow what you actually hear at home.
